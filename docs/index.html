Our story

A little cafe with a simple promise.

At KUKI, every cake, every loaf, every cup is made by us, for you, today. No yesterday's stock. No shortcuts. Just slow mornings, warm ovens, and the kind of care you can taste.

We're a family-run cafe in Sri Lanka, and we believe a clean kitchen is the first ingredient. Everything else follows.

How we bake

Clean kitchen. Honest ingredients. Every morning.

01

Spotless start

The day begins long before the doors open — deep-cleaning the kitchen, sanitising every surface and tool, and laying out the day's ingredients.

02

Real ingredients

Fresh butter, real eggs, good flour, real fruit. No mixes, no shortcuts. If we wouldn't eat it ourselves, we don't serve it.

03

Baked, not stored

Everything you see on the counter was made this morning. What doesn't sell doesn't come back tomorrow — your cake is always today's cake.

04

Hand-finished

Frostings, fillings, decorations — all done by hand, one cake at a time. Small batches, careful work.
